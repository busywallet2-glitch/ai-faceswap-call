## Short description
A consent-first camera studio for face tracking, experimental face effects and browser video calls.

## Full description
Busywallet Studio is an experimental camera and browser-calling workspace with face tracking and optional neural face replacement.

**Camera and tracking**
- Start the camera only when you choose.
- Track facial landmarks in a live preview.
- Switch between front and rear cameras where supported.
- Use portrait or landscape framing controls.

**Experimental face effects**
- Load an optional neural face-replacement engine in supported browsers and devices.
- Upload a portrait only when you have permission from the person shown.
- Review performance and model-loading status before relying on the output.

**Browser calling**
- Start or join a browser-to-browser video call using a call ID.
- Control camera and microphone during a call.
- Share the processed browser video output where supported.

**Important limitations**
This is an experimental prototype. Neural face replacement depends on browser, device, model availability, lighting and GPU performance. It is not guaranteed to work on every Android device. It is not integrated with WhatsApp or Signal and does not provide a system-wide virtual camera. Voice controls are audio effects, not voice identity cloning or true voice conversion. Captions and translation depend on browser and third-party services.

**Consent and responsible use**
Use only your own face or a face image whose subject has explicitly agreed. Tell call participants when video is altered. Do not use the app to impersonate someone, mislead viewers, bypass identity checks or create non-consensual content.
